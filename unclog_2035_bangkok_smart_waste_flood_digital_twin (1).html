Root-Cause Waste & Drainage Mechanics:

        
    
          
  • Bangkok's 6,400 km pipe network and 1,682 canals are rated for 60 mm/hour rainfall, but ~70,000 tonnes/year of waterway waste and 6,908 tonnes/year clogging pumping stations choke hydraulic flow.
    • 
          
  • Phra Khanong Pumping Station alone intercepts 15 tonnes/day of refuse, while street-food Fats, Oils, and Grease (FOG) solidify inside sub-surface drains.
    • 
          
  • UNCLOG 2035 automates source waste sorting, hydrolyzes FOG with AI-dosed lipase-producing bacteria, and converts organics into Scope 2 biogas electricity (powering all 158 pump stations) and pyrolysis biochar (lining 25 retention basins).
